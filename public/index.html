Your storage, your account

Files move through your PC
to the destinations you choose.

EverySync does not send your synchronized files to an EverySync-operated server. Cloud sign-in credentials are stored in an encrypted configuration on your Windows PC. Google Drive access includes permission to manage files; configured synchronizations use the folders you select.
